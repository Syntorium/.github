## About

UAE-based engineering and growth studio. Founded 2019 · Syntorium FZ-LLC · headquartered at SPC Free Zone, Sharjah · with a 24-person team distributed across MENA, EU, and US (async-first).

We design, ship, and operate the systems we build — and stay on them so they keep working. Most of the bugs we've been hired to fix lived in the seams between teams that didn't.

## What we ship

- **Backend platforms & ERPs** — Laravel + Vue + Inertia. POS, ERP, CRM, MetaTrader 5 back-offices, forex trading platforms.
- **Web apps** — Type-safe React / Vue stacks on Astro, Next, or Nuxt. Edge-deployed, instrumented, fast.
- **Mobile & desktop** — React Native, Flutter, Swift, Kotlin, Electron. iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux.
- **API & integrations** — REST, GraphQL, ERP / CRM / Microsoft Dynamics 365 glue, payment & identity flows.
- **WhatsApp Business** — Cloud API platforms with multi-agent chat dashboards, automated workflows, transactional pipelines.
- **E-commerce** — Shopify, Magento, Microsoft Dynamics 365 BC. Storefronts, OMS, the bridges between.
- **Cloud / DevOps** — Cloudflare, AWS, Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Vultr. CI / IaC / observability / on-call.
- **AI, automation & data extraction** — LLM features, RAG, agents, Puppeteer & Playwright at scale, SERP and Maps scraping.
- **WordPress** — headless or classic, scaled, hardened, and migrated when it's time.
- **UI / UX design** — design systems, product discovery, prototyping. Built to ship, not to print.
- **Digital marketing & SEO** — SEO, Meta / Google / TikTok ads, lifecycle, GMB, zero-to-digital launches.
- **Branding** — visual identity, voice, and the digital surfaces that bring them to life.
